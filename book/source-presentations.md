The four presentations supplied by James L. Tobias are primary project context. The notebooks translate their themes into new, small public teaching examples. The original PDFs are not redistributed: slides include third-party graphics, organizational references, and context-specific system details whose terms remain separate.

## Rewiring as a social and technical task

Zuckerman's *Rewire* emphasizes that communication technology does not by itself create meaningful cross-cultural connection. The practical question here is whether an analysis brings in knowledge and voices that the old workflow excluded. [Author's book description](https://ethanzuckerman.com/books/rewire/).

The supplied global-health Six Thinking Hats image adds a deliberative framing. Our original diagram and Lab 07 credit Edward de Bono's method, distinguish human parallel thinking from computational role prompts, and label purple synthesis as a project extension. No assistant role is treated as a substitute for community participation.

## What has been newly authored

The Python models, synthetic data, SVG explanations, map demo, and short silent video were created for this repository with AI-assisted development. They are inspectable teaching artifacts. The user-supplied splash graphic is retained unchanged. Authorship and generation do not establish the scientific validity of the illustrated relationships; independent validation is required before operational adaptation.
