Read the scene

Color encodes temperature on a fixed 20–38°C scale. Height = max(0, temperature − 20) × 150 display meters. This visual exaggeration is not a physical height.

Drag to pan. Use Shift + drag to rotate or tilt; the view buttons provide a simpler alternative. Click a column to inspect its value.
